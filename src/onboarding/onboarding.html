    How MUGA quiets your URLs
    Removes 450+ noise patterns from every URL fbclid, gclid, UTMs, and hundreds more, gone before the page loads. The cleaning runs locally in your browser, with no analytics and no telemetry.
    Unwraps the detours: AMP, ping beacons, redirect wrappers Every detour the web adds between you and the page you wanted is straightened out, locally, inside your browser.
    Clean URLs are shorter, prettier, and safe to share Sometimes you can barely tell where a link goes with all the noise attached. Right-click any link to copy it clean, quiet and honest.
    Aggressive privacy controls, off by default In Settings, under Advanced, you can also remove the referrer header and block tracking beacons on every site you visit. Both are advanced, off-by-default options: nothing changes unless you turn them on there.
    Short links, resolved locally

    Short links hide where they go. Links like bit.ly or t.co don't show their real destination. If you allow it, MUGA follows them right in your browser (no server in the middle) and strips the noise off wherever they land. You stay in control: it only applies to a short, known list of shorteners, and only if you turn it on in settings.

    MUGA does not send your data anywhere on its own. If you have browser sync enabled, your preferences sync through the browser (Google for Chrome, Firefox Accounts for Firefox). That's a browser feature, not MUGA.

    Built by learning from mistakes. MUGA grew through many iterations, and it won't be perfect. If you find a link it cleaned wrong, a referral it shouldn't have touched, or anything that feels off, please let us know. Questions, ideas, and bug reports are genuinely welcome, and they shape where MUGA goes next.
