    How MUGA quiets the web
    Removes 450+ noise patterns from every URL fbclid, gclid, UTMs, and hundreds more — gone before the page loads. Nothing is collected. Nothing is sent anywhere.
    Unwraps the detours: AMP, ping beacons, redirect wrappers Every detour the web adds between you and the page you wanted is straightened out, locally, inside your browser.

    How MUGA keeps the lights on

    Creators come first. If a creator's affiliate tag is already on a link, MUGA never touches it -- we don't take credit from people who earned it.

    On selected stores where a link has no affiliate tag at all, MUGA can add ours. Your price never changes. The code is open source, you can verify it.

    MUGA recognises both styles of affiliate attribution -- some programs put the creator's tag directly on the merchant's URL, others route the click through an affiliate network's own server. We respect both. Whoever earned the click keeps it.

    Short links, resolved locally

    Short links hide where they go. Links like bit.ly or t.co don't show their real destination. If you allow it, MUGA follows them right in your browser -- no server in the middle -- and strips the noise off wherever they land. You stay in control: it only applies to a short, known list of shorteners, and only if you turn it on in settings.

    Remote rule updates

    Your other device has remote rule updates enabled. With this on, MUGA performs a weekly fetch of a signed tracking-param list from a public GitHub Pages endpoint to keep your protection fresh. No user data is sent.

    MUGA never sends data anywhere. If you have browser sync enabled, your preferences sync through the browser (Google for Chrome, Firefox Accounts for Firefox) — that's a browser feature, not MUGA.
