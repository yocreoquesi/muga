What it does

450+ bits of noise, gone

UTMs, fbclid, gclid, e-commerce noise, share tokens, click IDs, email beacons. All removed before the page loads.

Never acts behind your back

You control everything. No analytics. No telemetry. Every feature is transparent, configurable, and open source.

Doesn't break things

Smart detection protects logins, OAuth flows, and payments. AMP redirect, redirect unwrapping, ping blocking built in.

Respectful of referrals

By default, we never replace another person's referral. If you want that, it takes a separate, deliberate choice. We don't touch what isn't ours unless you tell us to.

Works on Chrome. Today.

Built natively for Manifest V3, where Chrome is headed. While legacy cleaning extensions lose access, MUGA runs on the current standard.

EN + ES

Full bilingual support. English and Spanish, natively written. Not machine-translated.

We don't replace by default. We never hide. We always ask.

Some extensions manipulate your links without telling you. MUGA does the opposite: we don't touch what isn't ours unless you tell us to. Our affiliate support is optional, disclosed during onboarding, and only adds a referral to links that have none. The entire source code is public. Read it.
