What it does

Clean links that still work.

Cleaning starts the moment you install. Nothing to set up, and everything is one switch away in Settings.

01 · strip

Strips the clutter

Campaign tags, click IDs and mailing IDs come off the links you open, copy and share, before the page even loads.

utm_*, fbclid, gclid, mc_cid, msclkid and 356 more…
02 · keep

Keeps links working

Search terms, filters and settings the page uses stay where they are, so the clean link opens the same page. If a site ever misbehaves, pause MUGA there with one click.

utm_source=newsletter · size=m stays
03 · reveal

See where a link really goes

Redirect wrappers open the page they point at, and short links are followed in your browser, so you land on a clean address. On desktop, hover a link and hold still to preview its real destination.

go.example/x7Kp2 → shop.example/p/linen-shirt
04 · copy

Copy a clean link

Right-click a link and choose Copy clean link. Ctrl + C tidies the links in your selection too, so what you paste is short and clear.

In the browser

Yours

Built for you, not about you.

  • Free
  • Open source
  • No telemetry

No analytics and no account. The one thing MUGA fetches on its own is a weekly signed list of cleaning rules, with nothing about you in the request, and it is one switch to turn off. Every line is public under GPL v3.

Creator links: when a link already carries a creator's referral tag, MUGA tries to leave it in place by default, and it never adds a tag of its own. It is a best-effort intention, not a guarantee.
